The road ahead

A NEW BBQ CONCEPT FOR THE PORTLAND AREA.

Smoke & Road BBQ is an independent food trailer concept currently in development. Our plan is to operate a customized, fully electric rental trailer, with smoked meats prepared ahead of time in an approved commercial kitchen and finished for service on-site.

We’re exploring a permanent home in a welcoming food cart pod where people can enjoy great food together.
